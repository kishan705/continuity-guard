EPISODE 1: The One-Year Deal
[00:00] INT. ST. BRIGID'S HOSPITAL - NIGHT
[00:05] Tears stream down twenty-four-year-old night-shift nurse Harper's face as she stares at an unpaid bill for her seventeen-year-old sister Mia's emergency heart surgery.
[00:11] HARPER: Please, Mia needs this heart surgery tonight or she will not survive!
[00:17] A rugged man in oil-stained coveralls steps forward and places a cashier's check on the hospital counter.
[00:23] COLE: I am paying for Mia's surgery in full right now.
[00:29] INT. AUTO SHOP - NIGHT
[00:34] Thirty-two-year-old mechanic Cole Hayes wipes grease off his hands as Harper follows him into the small garage.
[00:40] HARPER: You are just a night-shift mechanic, Cole, how do you have one hundred thousand dollars for my sister?
[00:46] COLE: My condition is simple: sign a one-year contract marriage with me tonight, and the hospital operates immediately.
[00:52] HARPER: A contract marriage with a stranger just to save Mia's life?
[00:57] Cole slides a signed legal marriage document across the workbench toward her.
[01:03] COLE: Sign it, Harper, or I walk away and your sister loses her chance.
[01:09] INT. ST. BRIGID'S HOSPITAL - NIGHT
[01:14] Harper signs the marriage contract with shaking hands as seventeen-year-old Mia is rolled into the operating room.
[01:20] Cole secretly glances at his phone screen displaying a message from Whitmore Shipping regarding his hidden CEO status.
[01:26] COLE: Your sister is safe now, wife.
[01:31] High heels clack against the tile floor as Cole's wealthy stepmother Diane Whitmore enters with her twenty-nine-year-old son Jake.
[01:37] DIANE: What is a pathetic mechanic like you doing marrying a broke nurse in public?
